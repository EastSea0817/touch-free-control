Touch-free Control

Starting…

      1. Camera and light

      Sit in front of the camera. The light, distance and position checks above must all turn green.

      2. Your head range

      Turn your head to five dots: the centre and the four corners of the screen. Hold still at each one. The window fills the screen while you do this.

      3. How you click

      Try it now. A bar turns green when it passes the line. Use − / + until a real click passes and talking or blinking does not.

      4. Ready

      • Pause or resume: close both eyes for 2 seconds, or press Ctrl+Alt+P.
      • Re-center: look at the middle of the screen and press Ctrl+Alt+C.
      • While you hold a click gesture, the cursor stays still so the click lands where you aimed.
      Settings

      This computer is not Windows, so the real mouse is not moved (preview only).
