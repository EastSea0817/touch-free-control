Mouse control

Control your computer with your face

Move the mouse by turning your head. Click with a wink, an open mouth or raised eyebrows. Everything runs on this computer; the camera image is never saved or sent.

A student research prototype. Not a medical device.

Step 1 of 3

Camera & light

Sit in front of the camera so your whole face is visible. All three checks should turn green.

  • Light
  • Distance
  • Position
Step 2 of 3

Head range

Five dots appear: the centre and the four corners of the screen. Turn your head toward each dot and hold still until it fills. The window covers the screen while you do this.

Step 3 of 3

Click gestures

Choose how you click, then try it. The bar turns green when your gesture passes the line. Use − and + so a real click passes and talking or blinking does not.

Cursor & timing

How the mouse follows your head and how long gestures are held. Changes are saved straight away.
