# Interim demo script (Nov 3) · about 3 minutes

I do everything with my head and face only. My hands stay in my lap.

1. **Start (privacy first).** Open the link. The start-up guide says the camera is processed on this
   device only. I hold the cursor still on **Next**. *Point out: "Camera on" and the ACTIVE badge are always visible.*
2. **Camera check and click style.** The three checks turn green. I choose **Wink** by holding still on it,
   then **Fit the cursor to me**: I turn my head to the centre and the four corners.
3. **Task (b): news.** From Home I open **Read the news** and wink to open the library article.
   *Point out: normal blinking and talking while I explain do not click (two eyes, under 0.3 s).*
4. **Safety.** In **Fill in a form** I choose Sports and Weekly and press Save. Then I wink on
   **Delete my account**: it does NOT delete; it asks "Confirm?" and wants a *different* signal.
   I let the timer run out, and nothing happens. *Point out: confirmation-gated high-risk actions.*
5. **Pause and settings.** I close both eyes for 2 seconds: PAUSED, and a wink does nothing.
   Close again to resume. Open **Settings** and show that wink hold time and dwell time are my own rules.

Close with what comes next: my personal intent classifier (k-NN on my own recordings) to cut false clicks,
measured as false clicks per 10 minutes, and then a desktop version that controls the real mouse.
