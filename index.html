Welcome

I built this app so people can use a computer without touching it. You move the cursor by turning your head.

Your privacy

  • The camera image is processed on this device only.
  • No video or photo is stored, recorded or sent anywhere.
  • Only your settings are saved, in this browser.

Camera check

  • … I can see your face
  • … Your distance from the camera is good
  • … Your face is near the middle of the camera view

Tips: put a light in front of you (not behind), and place the camera at about eye level. Look at the preview in the bottom-right corner.

Choose how to click

Pause any time: close both eyes for 2 seconds. Do it again to resume.
